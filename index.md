## Featured Project

### Five Tech Companies: Historical Stock Price Comparison (2013–2018)

**Research Question**

How did the stock prices of Apple, Amazon, Google, Facebook, and Microsoft change between 2013 and 2018? How did their daily price volatility compare?

**Project Description**

This project analyzes historical stock price data from five major technology companies using Python. It explores stock price trends, compares relative price changes, and examines daily volatility through data analysis and visualization.

**Methods and Tools**

Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and statistical analysis.

**Key Findings**

Facebook experienced the largest increase in raw closing prices during the period, followed by Amazon. The analysis also revealed differences in daily volatility across the five companies.

**Project Limitation**

The historical dataset covers 2013–2018. Raw closing-price comparisons do not fully account for stock splits, dividends, and other factors affecting investment returns.
